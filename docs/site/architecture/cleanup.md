# Cleanup roadmap

gorge grew quickly, and alongside the engine the repository carries several
research programmes (search, learned policies, benchmark replications), some of
which have concluded. This page lists code that is redundant, parked or
optional, with the evidence and the recommended direction. It is a roadmap,
not a commitment: each item is decided on its own.

*Survey of `main` @ `f1ac002c4`, 2026-10-12; resolved items re-checked
2026-10-16 after the restructure (`6d95c6a54`). Confidence: **H** high,
**M** medium, **L** low.*

## Resolved by the restructure (2026-10-16)

[ADR 0001](https://github.com/adams-shaun/gorge/blob/main/docs/decisions/0001-repo-layout.md)
replaced the flat root with `engine/`, `bots/`, `server/`, `internal/`,
`cmd/` and `ops/` (plus a fenced `lab/` research tree that left gorge the
same day; see below), and closed these items:

- **Flat root**: done. The root now holds only the group directories and the
  top-level files.
- **`deck.list` and `benchmark.md`**: done; both are gone from the root (the
  `.gitattributes` rule for `deck.list` went with it).
- **Dead commands**: done. `cmd/autopayaudit` and `cmd/manamodelstats` are
  deleted, and the research binaries left gorge (below).
- **`seat` linking research code**: done. `engine/seat` is the `Seat`
  contract only; the default bot is `bots/heuristic`.
- **Historical design records**: parked out of the tree; see
  [`docs/design/ARCHIVE.md`](https://github.com/adams-shaun/gorge/blob/main/docs/design/ARCHIVE.md).

## Resolved by the companion-repository split (2026-10-16)

Draft events, the compliance oracles, training research, the research
binaries and scripts, and the research bots moved to a private companion
repository that imports gorge (gorge never imports it). Every item below that
named one of those paths is closed in gorge by that move, and is marked
*moved out*.

## Priorities

1. **Restore a zero-dependency `go.mod`** (H). Done: the purego-backed GPU
   scorer moved out of gorge, and `internal/archtest`
   `TestRootModuleHasNoRequires` fails on any root `require` line.
2. **Remove dead weight** (H). Done: `patches/`, `internal/hindsight` and
   `cmd/hindsight` are deleted, and the three `bots/sb*` wrappers were merged
   into one package, since moved out.
3. **Decouple the parked SpellBench stacks** (M). Done: front end V and the
   SpellBench v1 and v2 bridges (~21k lines) moved out; gorge keeps only the
   SpellBench deck catalogs and builtin bots (`bots/spellbench`).
4. **Regenerate the package map** (H) from `go list` doc strings, and correct the
   invariants document where it has drifted.
5. **Choose the surviving search harnesses** (L) on benchmark evidence.
